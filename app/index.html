    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions: story first, then the finale, then side strands and activities, then collectibles, then the 100% cleanup. One playthrough is enough — there are no difficulty trophies and no online trophies. Do not use cheats: they disable trophies and saving. Missions cannot be replayed, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapter 1. Play the main missions to the end of Chapter 1. Meditate at the first meditation spot the moment it is offered (see Missables). Earn: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, Trust.
    2. Stage 2 — Story, Chapter 2. Both protagonists are playable now and the world opens up. Start side strands and activities as they appear. Earn: T**s Ass Drug Money, Break the Bank, Family Matters.
    3. Stage 3 — Story, Chapter 3. Keep the story moving and keep clearing stranger strands. Earn: Vice City, Baby!, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked.
    4. Stage 4 — Story, Chapter 4. Last full chapter. Before the finale, check the missables list and make a manual save. Earn: Top Of The World, Ma, Snow Storm.
    5. Stage 5 — Finale. Complete the final mission. Earn: You Gotta Know When to Get Off (gold), Actions Speak Louder.
    6. Stage 6 — Side characters & strangers. Finish every stranger strand (8 strands for the silver) and the final mission for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus Bret's hunt and every meditation spot.
    7. Stage 7 — Activities & challenges. Races (20 wins), drone races, shooting range golds (42 challenges), 250 stunts, 50 challenges, robberies (shops, bank vaults, armored trucks, gang operations), and the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent).
    8. Stage 8 — Collectibles & exploration. Card sets (3), WAiNK It at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, all garages bought, 10 vehicles registered, all of Leonida explored.
    9. Stage 9 — Cleanup & 100%. Work the in-game 100% checklist (story, robberies, gang operations, collectibles, races, stunts, challenges, discoveries) until A Long Road (gold) pops — then the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, follows automatically.

    Missable trophies — read this first

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the launch guides (PowerPyx and GTABase). Everything else on the list is earned by playing the story, repeating an activity, or exploring — but because missions cannot be replayed, a manual save at the start of every chapter is your safety net.

    Watch list: story-choice trophy Actions Speak Louder involves a decision — save before major story decisions. All other trophies are unmissable by the official descriptions.
